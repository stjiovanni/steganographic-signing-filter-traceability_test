# Caldelli et al. (2017) - Image origin classification

**Harvard reference:** Caldelli, R., Becarelli, R. and Amerini, I. (2017) 'Image origin classification based on social network provenance', *IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security*, 12(6), pp. 1299-1308.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/TIFS.2017.2656842

**Verification:** DOI resolution returned the IEEE publication metadata, including the 2017 issue, pages, title, and authors.

**Relevance:** Connects image forensics with social-network provenance and source classification. It supports discussing provenance as a separate forensic inference rather than equating a low perceptual-hash distance with provenance.
