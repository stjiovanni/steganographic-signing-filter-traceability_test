# Ahmadi et al. (2020) - ReDMark

**Harvard reference:** Ahmadi, M., Norouzi, A., Soroushmehr, S.M.R., Karimi, N., Najarian, K., Samavi, S. and Emami, A. (2020) ‘ReDMark: Framework for residual diffusion watermarking based on deep networks’, *Expert Systems with Applications*, 146, article 113132.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2020.113132

**Official record:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0957417419308759

**Use in FPR:** Provides contextual support for learned residual watermarking and robustness across distortions. ReDMark is not evaluated in this project.
