# Windisch et al. (2024) - Hadamard Error-Correcting Codes

**Harvard reference:** Windisch, M., Wassermann, J., Leba, M. and Stoicuta, O. (2024) ‘Hadamard error-correcting codes and their application in digital watermarking’, *Sensors*, 24(10), article 3062.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/s24103062

**Official record:** https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/24/10/3062

**Use in FPR:** Supports future error-correction ablations comparing raw BER, corrected payload recovery, and false-positive behaviour. Hadamard coding is not implemented here.
